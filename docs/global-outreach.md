# Hormoz Intelligence — Global Outreach Kit

Hormoz is an independent, early-stage human-centered technology project. Nava, its assistant, is experimental and the project is still being developed. We welcome thoughtful feedback and voluntary collaboration; no paid role or funding is promised.

**Public home:** https://tarahikhalil-jpg.github.io/hormoz/

## English
🌍 **Hormoz Intelligence — Technology for people, everywhere**

Hormoz is an independent, early-stage project exploring how technology can help people learn, share skills, collaborate, and find new opportunities—regardless of their language, country, or financial means. Nava, its AI assistant, is experimental and the project is still under development.

We welcome developers, AI researchers, designers, translators, educators, and anyone with constructive ideas. This is an invitation to learn about the project and contribute voluntarily; paid work is not promised.

“Technology should serve people—not the other way around.”

## हिन्दी

Hormoz एक स्वतंत्र और शुरुआती चरण का प्रोजेक्ट है, जिसका उद्देश्य तकनीक के ज़रिए लोगों को सीखने, अपने कौशल साझा करने, सहयोग करने और नए अवसर खोजने में मदद करना है—चाहे उनकी भाषा, देश या आर्थिक स्थिति कुछ भी हो। इसका AI सहायक Nava अभी प्रयोगात्मक है और प्रोजेक्ट पर काम जारी है।
